## 🌍 Why Does the World Need This?

Look around you. The modern web is suffering from a collective identity crisis.

Every single landing page looks like it was designed by a minimalist Swiss monk who took a vow of emotional suppression:
- **Inter**, **Roboto**, **Helvetica Neue** everywhere.
- Stark corporate minimalism screaming: *"We are very serious B2B SaaS disruptors."*
- Terms of Service written in fonts specifically engineered to induce rapid REM sleep.
- Stock tickers and breaking news giving you an ulcer in razor-sharp sans-serif.

**Enough is enough.**

The world doesn't need another clean, sterile, neo-grotesque typeface. The world needs **warmth**. The world needs **joy**. The world needs the visual equivalent of an enthusiastic puppy knocking over a lamp.

The world needs **Comic Sans**.

---

## 💡 The Groundbreaking Science

Peer-reviewed studies by scientists who we just made up prove that:

- **Bills & Overdue Notices** look 73% less threatening when rendered in bubbly, kindergarten-grade typography.
- **Corporate HR memos** lose all disciplinary authority when written in Comic Sans.
- **Existential dread** is scientifically impossible when reading Nietzsche in a font meant for a cartoon dog talking in a comic strip.
- **Dyslexia friendliness**: Actually, no joke here—Comic Sans has distinct letter shapes that genuinely help many people read more easily. Take *that*, typography snobs!

## 🚀 Installation

Because Google won't let us submit this to the Chrome Web Store without a 4-week ethical review, install it as an unpacked extension:

1. Clone or download this repo:
   ```bash
   git clone https://git.p20.dev/christian/comic-sansify.git
   ```
2. Open Chrome and head over to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions/
   ```
3. Enable **Developer mode** (toggle in the top-right corner).
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left).
5. Select the `comic-chrome` folder.
6. Pin the extension to your toolbar, navigate to LinkedIn, and watch the corporate dystopia crumble into whimsical giggles.
